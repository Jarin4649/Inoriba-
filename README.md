# Inoriba-祈り場
Unfolds as a young child disappears without a trace. Frustrated by the slow and ineffective police investigation, the desperate parent decides to investigate the case on their own. Guided by unsettling rumors and cryptic clues, the protagonist explores forgotten locations, uncovers hidden truths, and ventures deeper into the darkness.
